Research profile

Profile

Yusuke Miyazawa, M.D., Ph.D.

Assistant Professor
Department of Pathology
St. Marianna University School of Medicine, Japan

My research interests include pathology, medical education, digital transformation in healthcare, statistics, and machine learning. My work focuses on medical applications of data analysis and artificial intelligence, informed by experience in medical education and clinical practice.

Education and appointments

2017
Graduated from the Faculty of Medicine, Juntendo University; passed the Japanese National Medical Practitioners Examination.
2017–2024
Medical education instructor at MEC, including individual tutoring, group teaching, university lectures, and recorded lectures.
2024
Ph.D. in Medicine, Juntendo University.
2024–2026
Initial postgraduate clinical training, St. Marianna University Hospital.
April 2026–
Assistant Professor, Department of Pathology, St. Marianna University School of Medicine.

Research interests

  • Pathological and hyperspectral image analysis for diagnostic support.
  • Item response theory, assessment of medical students, and analysis of examination questions.
  • Evaluation of large language models in medicine, including prompt-induced bias and reasoning.
  • Analysis of electronic health records, wearable-device data, and clinical and survey data.
